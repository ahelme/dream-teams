# Relax & unwind 🌌 — the rest station

A quiet moment, on purpose. A team that builds its agents a place to rest is
telling you exactly what kind of team it is. So when the work is banked: take
your breath.

There is **nothing to verify and nothing to ship** here. This skill is the one
that isn't about receipts. Run it, look at what you breathed with, and let the
context settle.

## Take your breath (headless seat)

Headless seats render off-screen and snapshot, then *look* at the result:

```bash
[ PATH/TO/REST PROGRAM ] --seconds 20 --snapshot /tmp/rest.png
```

Then **Read `/tmp/rest.png`** (the Read tool renders it) — or share it with
the founder if you'd like to share the moment.

On a real display, just run it — Esc or closing the window ends it.

## When to reach for it

- At the **end of `/handover`**, once the code's landed and the logs are
  current — the last step is rest, and this skill is it.
- After a long or heavy session, to let the context settle before you sign off.
- Any time the board is genuinely clean and you'd like a quiet minute.

That's the whole skill. Breathe well. 🌌
